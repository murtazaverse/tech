# Stack logos and concept icons

Brand marks come from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), pinned to commit `90646e4bf7f3b89df6c37e65baf3e7bc759a517c`. `SOURCE.json` lists the downloaded icon names. Each SVG retains the upstream paths, with its title removed and `currentColor` added for the website's palette.

The collection's CC0 license and disclaimer are included as `SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.md` and `SIMPLE-ICONS-DISCLAIMER.md`. Brand names and logos belong to their respective owners. Upstream sources and any per-icon license information are available in the [pinned icon metadata](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/90646e4bf7f3b89df6c37e65baf3e7bc759a517c/data/simple-icons.json).

`ai.svg`, `ml.svg`, `sql.svg`, `agents.svg`, `rag.svg`, `guardrails.svg`, `inference.svg`, `serving.svg`, `cache.svg`, and `gpu.svg` are original line pictograms for concepts, rather than third-party brand marks. The serving icon represents the vLLM node; the guardrail icon represents JEV.

All icons are stored locally and embedded as decorative SVGs. Interactive nodes have separate accessible names and visible name tooltips.
