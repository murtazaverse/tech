# Space photography and artwork

- `saturn.jpg`: **Saturn, Approaching Northern Summer** (PIA21046). Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech/Space Science Institute. [NASA source](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/saturn-approaching-northern-summer/).
- `pluto.jpg`: **Pluto in True Color** (PIA19857). Credit: NASA/Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory/Southwest Research Institute. [NASA source](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/pluto-in-true-color/).
- `earth.jpg`: **The Blue Marble**, Apollo 17 photograph AS17-148-22727. Credit: NASA Johnson Space Center. [NASA source](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/the-blue-marble/).
- `mars.jpg`: **Mars Planet Globe**, a mosaic of Viking Orbiter images. Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech. [NASA source](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/mars-planet-globe/).
- `milky-way-nasa.jpg` (active background): **Milky Way on the Horizon**, photographed January 29, 2025. Credit: **NASA/Don Pettit**. [NASA source](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/milky-way-on-the-horizon/), [NASA media usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). CSS crops the starfield to exclude the bright terrestrial horizon, then applies rotation, dimming, and a soft mask.
- `milky-way.jpg` (retained, unused): **The Milky Way panorama** (eso0932a). Credit: **ESO/S. Brunier**. [ESO source](https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/), [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). This earlier background is no longer displayed by the website.

The photographs are stored locally at web display sizes. Partial visibility, darkening, blending, and the scrolling presentation are applied in CSS; the downloaded image files are unmodified. Scrolling reveals the four planet encounters in sequence. Their placement is decorative, not an astronomical distance scale.

`asteroid.svg`, the earlier unused `milky-way.svg` texture, and the inline rocket, satellite, and UFO vectors are original decorative artwork created for this website. Rockets and UFOs have attached exhaust; satellites do not.

The asteroids are plain, without stack marks. Logos used in the overview and skills graph are documented in [the stack asset credits](../stack/CREDITS.md). The section illustrations in `templates/illustrations/` are original SVG artwork. The contact portrait uses the existing user-supplied `assets/img/profile.jpeg` photograph.
