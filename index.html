Master's Thesis · Research

Smart LLM Routing & Placement Layer

Hasso Plattner Institute (HPI) / University of Potsdam

LLM inference runs on power-hungry GPUs, yet not every request needs maximum compute. I built a placement layer on top of vLLM that predicts how heavy an answer will be from the prompt alone, then routes the request to the most cost-effective GPU tier available.

The novel contribution: wiring output-length prediction directly into hardware-tier selection across a heterogeneous GPU fleet — a decision usually made blindly.

  • vLLM
  • LLM Inference
  • Heterogeneous GPUs
  • Scheduling
  • SLO-aware Serving
  • Output predictor. Estimates response length and compute weight before generation begins.
  • Tier-aware routing. Sends light requests to cheaper GPUs, heavy ones to high-end accelerators.
  • Cost & energy savings. Cuts serving cost and power draw versus uniform placement.
  • SLO-preserving. Meets strict response-time targets while doing it.
